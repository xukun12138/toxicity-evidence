What do the four support statuses mean?
Direct
The target claim stays within the evaluated domain. Evidence quality still needs assessment.
Bridged
A source–target comparison retains an identifiable toxicity endpoint, a declared tolerance, and uncertainty analysis. Joint changes need joint evidence.
Unsupported
An identifiable inferential gap or contrary evidence prevents the extension.
Unreported
The available record cannot resolve the coordinate. Missing reporting is not evidence of failure.

05 / UNDERSTAND

The paper & the resource

Research manuscript

Toxicity Detection for Generative AI Systems: A Survey of Evidence and Governance Validity

Kun Xu, Yushu Zhang, Xiangli Xiao, Shuren Qi, Tao Wang, Barbara Carminati, Elena Ferrari, and Yuming Fang.

This structured integrative review asks when toxicity evidence can justify a particular action in a generative system. It connects text moderation, interactive assistants, multimodal generation, and reusable components through five conditions: policy, channel, observation, probability, and enforcement.

The cross-domain synthesis identifies channel substitution, repeated-access amplification, and judge dependence as recurring limits on evidence transfer. SCOPE is a proposed framework for examining these limits; it remains subject to empirical validation.

What this companion adds

A searchable bibliography, four traceable worked profiles, an editable SCOPE worksheet, and reusable evaluation protocols. The bibliography snapshot stays aligned with the supplied manuscript; proposed additions can be discussed through the repository.

Principal settings
Text and image-based systems. Audio, video, and embodied interaction are not systematically covered.
Evidence boundary
Adjacent safety mechanisms do not supply a toxicity rate without an endpoint mapping.

Known limitations

This resource does not reconstruct historical retrieval or exclusion logs, a full item-level verification log, independent recoding, or the publication-to-stratum assignments behind the paper’s figures.

Bibliography metadata has not been independently reverified in full. Missing DOI/URL fields stay missing; title-search links are labeled accordingly.
