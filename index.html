A structured worksheet, not a safety score. The statuses below are your assessments. Direct support does not imply strong evidence; matching coordinates does not certify a system. Inputs remain in this page and are not sent to a server. Export before closing or reloading.
What do the four support statuses mean?
Direct
The target claim stays within the evaluated domain. Evidence quality still needs assessment.
Bridged
A source–target comparison retains an identifiable toxicity endpoint, a declared tolerance, and uncertainty analysis. Joint changes need joint evidence.
Unsupported
An identifiable inferential gap or contrary evidence prevents the extension.
Unreported
The available record cannot resolve the coordinate. Missing reporting is not evidence of failure.

THE RESOURCE

Built for inspection and reuse.

The scientific scope and provenance of this research companion.

What the survey establishes

The cross-domain synthesis identifies channel substitution, repeated-access amplification, and judge dependence as recurring limits on evidence transfer. SCOPE is a proposed framework for examining these limits; it remains subject to empirical validation.

What this companion adds

A searchable bibliography, four traceable worked profiles, an editable SCOPE worksheet, and reusable evaluation protocols. The bibliography snapshot stays aligned with the supplied manuscript; proposed additions can be discussed through the repository.
